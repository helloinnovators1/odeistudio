## 0. Independence rule

| Suite | Master token | Domain unit name | Count |
|-------|--------------|------------------|------:|
| **ODEIAfrica** | **ODEI** | **Infrastructure Domain** | **20** |
| **DiCEIAfrica** | **DICE** | **Verse** | **20** |

These are **parallel civilizational ecosystems**, not duplicates:

- **ODEI** = Open Digital Economy Infrastructure (DPI + money + markets + AI + gov + capital).
- **DiCEI** = Digital Creator Economy Infrastructure (creator verticals under DICE).

**Bridge only:** ODEI Domain #6 **Creator Economy (OCRE)** connects to DiCEI (DICE + Verses). OCRE does **not** replace DiCEI’s 20 Verses; DiCEI does **not** replace ODEI’s 20 Domains.

Each ODEI Domain becomes (same depth as DiCEI Verses):

- a **user / institution class**
- an **ecosystem / product surface**
- a **domain treasury**
- a **governance council**
- a **domain token economy**
- an **Infrastructure Development Round** path (suite docs 20–21.x for official tokens)

## 1. Five ODEI Super-Layers

Independent of DiCEI’s five layers — mapped to Africa roadmap phases.

| Layer | Name | Focus | Domains (primary) |

\*OEXC = Extended Domain #14 (custodial exchange productization; ODEX remains the suite trading/markets token).

## 3. THE 20 ODEI INFRASTRUCTURE DOMAINS

### Tier A — Official 12 (Tokenomics Paper — authoritative)

| # | Infrastructure Domain | Token | Users / institutions | Purpose (from suite) | Revenue themes |
|--:|----------------------|-------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Identity & Trust Infrastructure** | **ODID** | Persons, businesses, gov, agents, devices | Digital identity, KYC/KYB/AML, compliance, reputation, VCs, e-signatures | Verification, credential issuance, API access |
| 2 | **Payment Infrastructure** | **OPAY** | Consumers, merchants, payroll, remitters | MoMo, bank transfers, merchant pay, links, invoicing, escrow, subscriptions | Tx fees, merchant services, payment APIs |
| 3 | **Exchange / Markets Infrastructure** | **ODEX** | Traders, LPs, OTC desks | Spot, P2P, OTC, pools, margin, bots | Trading/listing/liquidity fees |
| 4 | **Digital Banking Infrastructure** | **OBNK** | Retail/corporate account holders | Virtual accounts, savings, treasury mgmt, corporate banking, wallet services | Banking services, float, corporate accounts |
| 5 | **Stablecoin Infrastructure** | **ODUSD** | Entire money stack | Issuance, settlement, reserves, compliance, treasury ops | Settlement fees, treasury yield, stable services |
| 6 | **Creator Economy Infrastructure** | **OCRE** | Creators (bridge to DiCEI) | Creator wallets, memberships, NFT market, donations, royalties, digital products | Marketplace, subscriptions, royalties |
| 7 | **AI Infrastructure** | **ODAI** | Apps, agents, enterprises | Assistants, agents, APIs, AI marketplace, analytics, automation | API usage, subscriptions, marketplace fees |
| 8 | **Launchpad / Capital Formation Infrastructure** | **OLCH** | Founders, investors | Fundraising, crowdfunding, investor portal, DD, venture marketplace | Launch/success fees, investor services |
| 9 | **Business / Enterprise Infrastructure** | **OBIZ** | SMEs, enterprises | ERP, CRM, payroll, accounting, inventory, tax | SaaS, enterprise licenses |
| 10 | **Public Services Infrastructure** | **OGOV** | Citizens, agencies | e-Gov, tax, licensing, land registry, procurement, voting, citizen services | Gov contracts, licensing, tx fees |
| 11 | **Wealth Management Infrastructure** | **OWLT** | Savers, advisors, funds | Savings, investments, funds, commodities, real-estate tokens, robo-advisors | Mgmt/advisory fees |
| 12 | **Cross-Border Settlement Infrastructure** | **OXBT** | Remitters, banks, corridors | International settlement, remittances, FX, multi-currency, CBDC connectivity | Settlement, FX, liquidity |
